## The duck on the desk

Rubber duck debugging is simple. You explain your code, line by line, to a rubber duck. The duck
is not expected to understand. You are.

## Why it works

Reading code in your head lets you skim past the part you assume is right. Saying it out loud
forces every step into words, and the faulty assumption has nowhere to hide.

## Why a duck and not a colleague

A colleague interrupts, suggests and gets pulled into your problem. A rubber duck listens, floats
calmly and never asks why you did not write a test first.

## Try it today

Next time you are stuck, find a duck, any duck, and start at the beginning. Squeak if you need to.
