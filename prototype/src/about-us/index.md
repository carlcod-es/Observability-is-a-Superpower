## Our story

Rubber Duck Consulting started life on a bathroom shelf, where the first duck spent years
listening to developers explain their bugs. Nobody ever heard a reply, yet the bugs kept fixing
themselves. We turned that into a business. We have heard it all, and we still listen.

## What we value

- **Patience.** We sit with you for as long as the explanation takes.
- **Confidentiality.** What you tell the duck stays with the duck.
- **Calm.** Floating steadily, even in hot water.

## The team

A flock of rubber ducks, led by Captain Squeak. Our debugging ducks sit on desks, our workshop
ducks line up along the tub and our retainer duck says nothing at all.
